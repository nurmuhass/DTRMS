# HR administration guide

Use the HR Manager workspace to maintain departments, eligible HODs, staff, supervisors and authorized HR Officer accounts. Choose a department before adding staff. Assign an active supervisor and supply a reason when changing assignments. Past decisions keep their original approver.

Use account status controls to deactivate/reactivate users while preserving historical reports. Verify the person's identity before resetting their password and use an approved delivery channel. Download the current Excel template for bulk import. Keep the four template columns as plain text and limit each import to 50 staff rows. Correct validation problems before retrying.

Open **Weekly schedules** to review staff-selected dates and to correct, unlock, approve or cancel a schedule with a recorded reason. Earlier fixed-day assignments remain in the historical system. [Weekly schedule instructions](docs/flexible-weekly-scheduling.md)

Open **Staff leave** to register leave and supporting documents, review pending records and see approved leave on the calendar. Approved leave removes the affected scheduled days from submission expectations. Record a reason for an overlap authorization, rejection or cancellation. [Leave instructions](docs/staff-leave.md)

Open **Submission monitoring** to review weekly or monthly compliance, overdue submissions, leave and approval status. Use the staff drilldown for daily reports and history, or configure holidays from the monitoring page. [Submission monitoring guide](docs/submission-monitoring.md)

Use the HR queue to review supervisor-approved reports. Review permission lets HR Officers inspect records. Final decisions require a separate grant, initially given to HR Managers. Check the active revision and supervisor decision. Return with a reason when correction is required. Resubmission restarts supervisor review.

Use report filters and exports according to assigned permissions. Pending means a submitted report still needs a decision. Missing means an expected report was unsubmitted for the selected period and roster. See [reporting definitions and export limits](docs/reporting-and-exports.md), the [organization workflow](docs/hr-management.md) and [final review](docs/hr-final-approval.md).
