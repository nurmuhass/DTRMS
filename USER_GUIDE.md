# User guide

Use the institution's approved HTTPS address and change your temporary password at first sign-in. Contact the authorized help desk if you need account recovery. Sign out when finished, especially on a shared device.

## Staff

Open **My Weekly Work Schedule** before Monday to confirm any three eligible dates for the next reporting week. Schedules lock when the week begins. Submit reports for your chosen dates by Sunday at 11:59 p.m. in the organization's timezone. Use the weekly record to see which dates have already passed, what is outstanding and whether approved leave applies. Contact HR for a schedule correction after Monday. [Weekly scheduling guide](docs/flexible-weekly-scheduling.md)

Open Staff and start a report. The account supplies your name, staff ID and department. Enter the date, arrival, start and end times, work details, achievements and challenges. Save a draft to finish later. Complete the declaration before submitting. You can submit one report per date and need an active supervisor assignment.

Use My Reports to track decisions and comments. Open a returned entry, apply corrections and resubmit it for supervisor review. The system preserves previous revisions and decisions. Open your report to download available PDF and Excel files. The notification menu shows new activity and lets you mark it read.

Attendance reflects staff-entered times. Overnight entries require an eligible schedule.

## Supervisors and HODs

Open the Supervisor queue, check submitted data and earlier revisions, then approve or return with a correction reason. Approval forwards the report to HR. Use approval history and the assigned-staff list for follow-up. Supervisor access follows role permissions and assignments.

See the [staff workflow](docs/staff-reporting.md) and [supervisor workflow](docs/supervisor-approval.md).
